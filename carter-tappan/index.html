The market, plainly

What I tell clients on the first call.

  1. Pricing is positioning.

    In 94301 the list price is a strategy, not a value. I'll show you the comps behind every dollar before we commit.

  2. Disclosure is leverage.

    Palo Alto buyers read reports like engineers. We front-load inspections so nothing surfaces at week three.

  3. Neighborhoods decide value.

    School corridors, walk streets, flood zones — the lot line matters as much as the floor plan.

  4. Momentum wins bidding wars.

    First-open-weekend traffic tells us everything. We plan around it, not after it.

Specialty

Mid-century homes need a mid-century advocate.

Eichlers and post-&-beam houses confuse generic agents: radiant heat, panelized walls, cathedral ceilings. I've walked hundreds across Greenmeadow, Fairmeadow and the Green Gables tracts — and I know which original details to protect and which upgrades appraise.

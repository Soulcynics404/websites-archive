Our story

Built on bacon & stubbornness.

One family, one griddle, and a firm belief that nobody should start the day on an empty plate.

Where it started

A counter, a griddle, a plan.

In 2008 we took over a tired old sandwich shop halfway down Bromley High Street. The oven was older than our daughter. The regulars were loyal to it anyway.

We kept the regulars, replaced the oven, and put everything we had into one thing: a full English done properly, every single plate. Word travels fast when the bacon's that good — these days 351 Google reviews do the talking for us.

Milestones

Eighteen years, one griddle spirit.

2008

The doors open

We serve 14 breakfasts on day one. Eleven go to builders from the site opposite who still come in on Fridays.

2012

The Full Stonehenge is born

A customer jokes the big breakfast "looks like it took a whole civilisation to build". The name sticks by lunchtime.

2017

Second generation behind the grill

Our daughter finishes catering college and comes home to run the coffee machine — then the whole floor.

2021

Veggie gets its own monument

The Veggie Stonehenge launches after years of requests. It outsells expectations within a month.

Today

351 reviews and counting

Same High Street spot, same recipes, same promise: if you leave hungry, that's on us — tell us and we'll fix it.

Come judge the fry-up for yourself — first refill of tea's on the house.

Our Method

Classical roots, modern bodies

Better Posture Pilates was built on a simple observation: most people don't need harder workouts — they need better ones. We teach Joe Pilates' classical order on contemporary Balanced Body equipment, adapted to how you actually live and work.

Certified instruction, continuing education every year, and an obsession with the small cues that make big changes.

Start with an assessment

Who we help

Desk-bound professionals, postpartum parents, runners and cyclists, anyone told to “fix their posture.”

Equipment

Reformer, Cadillac, Wunda Chair and barrels — a full classical studio under one roof.

Getting started

Your first session is a 60-minute posture assessment and intro workout. No experience needed.

Notre histoire

Our Story

Two families, one stubborn belief: a great café is really a small theatre of small pleasures — warm bread, honest coffee, a table where nobody rushes you.

Chapter one

A corner of South End, a corner of France

Le Bon Cafe began with a suitcase of recipes, a second-hand deck oven, and the slightly mad idea that Croydon deserved a proper French boulangerie-café. Not a themed imitation — the real thing: croissants laminated over two days, baguettes from an overnight poolish, coffee taken as seriously as in any Parisian arrondissement.

Today we're proud to be rated 4.5 stars by more than 450 neighbours. Some come for the morning croissant ritual, some for the lunchtime baguette queue, some just to read the paper under the gold-and-burgundy lights. Everyone gets the same welcome: bonjour.

Chapter two

Mornings start at 4am. The bread insists.

The ovens click on before the birds. Dough that rested overnight is shaped by hand, baked, and on the shelf by six-thirty. Whatever doesn't sell goes to the community fridge down the road — day-old bread should feed people, not bins.

Ce qu'on croit

What we believe

🧈

Real butter or nothing

Normandy butter in every lamination. It costs more. It tastes more. There is no version of this café without it.

⏳

Time is an ingredient

Slow ferments, long proofs. Bread made in a hurry tastes like hurry, and we'd rather close than serve that.

🤝

Regulars are family

We remember orders, hold tables, slip the odd extra cornichon. A café is its people before its pastries.

🌍

Bonjour means everyone

Moroccan mint tea beside French cocoa beside English builder's tea — our counter reflects our street, and we love it.

“We didn't want to open a French café in London. We wanted to open ours.”
— Le Bon Cafe, since day one

Our story

Six tables under a fig tree

Fig Tree Cafe began in 2016 with a borrowed espresso machine, a family recipe book and a garden that refused to stay tidy. Today we serve all-day brunch and cold-pressed juice to London's hungriest garden-lovers — but the soil is still ours, and so is the welcome.

What we stand for

Three simple promises

🌱 Grown, not ordered in

Herbs, salads and edible flowers come from our own beds steps from the kitchen. If we can't grow it, we buy it from growers within fifty miles.

🍳 Cooked like family's watching

Mum's shakshuka recipe, Nan's crumble topping, our own sourdough starter named Gerald. Nothing leaves the pass that we wouldn't eat ourselves.

🤝 Friendly is non-negotiable

We learn your name by your second visit and your usual by the third. Dogs, prams and messy handwriting on birthday cards all welcome.

How we got here

From seedling to supper club

2016

The first six tables

Rosa and Eli open a weekend coffee stand among their allotment fig trees. It rains. People come anyway.

2018

The kitchen arrives

A proper brunch menu launches — pancakes, shakshuka and the fry-up that still anchors weekends.

2021

Juice at dawn

The cold press moves in. Fresh juices become half of everything we serve, and the Green Reset earns cult status.

2024

The garden grows up

Fifty covers, a covered winter terrace, and 436 glowing Google reviews — each one pinned to the staff noticeboard.

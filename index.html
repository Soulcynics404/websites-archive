Our Story

A family table that grew into a Croydon institution

Galicia began as a family's love letter to the food of Spain — recipes carried over from Galician kitchens, cooked the way they always have been. Decades on, we're still family-run, still shopping the markets each morning, and still convinced that the best meal is the one you linger over.

The kitchen turns out the classics done properly: sizzling gambas al ajillo, silk-smooth croquetas, pulpo on wooden plates, and paella made to order in the pan — never rushed, never from a bag.

  • ✓Fresh daily — market fish, produce and charcuterie sourced each morning
  • ✓Cooked to order — paella and grill dishes made when you order them
  • ✓Everyone welcome — long lunches, family tables, celebrations big and small

Welcome to Martil

A proper neighbourhood cafe, done properly.

Martil Cafe has been serving Morland Road long before it was cool. Everything is cooked fresh to order — breakfasts that actually fill you up, coffee pulled by people who care, and cakes baked in-house when they last (they rarely do).

Halal-friendly kitchen, veggie options done with real thought, and prices that let you come back tomorrow. That's why 542 people took the time to review us — and why we're the highest rated cafe around.

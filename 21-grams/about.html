Our Story

Built on 21 grams of obsession

They say the soul weighs 21 grams. We think that’s roughly what it takes to make an espresso worth crossing town for.

Chapter One · 2019

A corner of King Street, stripped back to the brick

We found the room by accident — a tired old shop unit at 308 King Street with good bones, bad lighting and a door at the back nobody could explain. Six weeks of dust later, the brass was up, the machine was in, and the first shot was pulled for a neighbour who still sits in the window seat every Thursday.

The idea never changed: weigh everything, waste nothing, and treat a Tuesday-morning flat white with the same seriousness as a Saturday full house. Every gram counts — on the scale, on the plate, in the welcome.

Chapter Two · The Garden

The door at the back

Remember that door nobody could explain? It leads to the secret garden — fifty feet of open air strung with fairy lights, buried under greenery, hidden from King Street entirely. It’s where proposals have happened, where babies have been named, and where the midweek regulars pretend it’s still the weekend.

In summer it runs on cold brew and long lunches. In winter, blankets come out and the hot chocolate gets a shot of orange zest. Either way: push through. Everyone does, eventually.

What We Stand On

Three things we’ll never budge on

⚖

Weighed, not guessed

Doses timed and weighed to the gram, every basket, every shot. If a coffee leaves the bar imperfect, it doesn’t leave at all.

✦

Sourced with names attached

Farms, not faceless blends. Our roasters rotate single origins seasonally and pay well above commodity — and you can taste the difference.

Regulars, not transactions

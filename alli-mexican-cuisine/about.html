Nuestra historia

Cooking like it's Sunday at home

From one comal to a full mesa

Alli began with a single comal, a cast-iron molcajete, and family recipes carried across generations. No shortcuts, no mixes — just corn nixtamalized overnight, chiles toasted by hand, and stocks that start before sunrise.

We cook the food our families actually eat: regional dishes from across Mexico, made with Texas-grown produce and Gulf seafood whenever we can get it.

  • Tortillas pressed to order, all day long
  • Moles and adobos built from scratch, never from a jar
  • Salsas ground fresh every morning
  • Kitchen and dining room run by family
“If it isn't good enough for our own table, it doesn't leave the kitchen.”
— The Alli family

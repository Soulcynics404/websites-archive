How it works

Eat like a Georgian sailor.

  1. Tear. Rip off one end of the boat. Don't be shy.
  2. Paddle. Stir the egg and butter through the cheese until it turns to velvet.
  3. Dip. Bread end in, swirl, lift, repeat until the boat is dry.
  4. Fight for the crust. The crispy bottom belongs to whoever's quickest.

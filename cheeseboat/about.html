Our story

From the Black Sea to Bedford Ave.

Khachapuri is Georgia's national dish — bread boats baked with cheese until the edges blister and the middle turns to molten gold. We brought it to Williamsburg and never looked back.

Why a boat?

Because the shape isn't a gimmick — it's engineering. The raised ends hold the cheese lake steady while the thin bottom crisps against the oven floor. Tear one end off and you're holding the perfect dipping paddle.

We bake every boat to order in a deck oven that runs all day. Nothing sits. Nothing re-heats. When it lands on your counter it's bubbling, and when you swirl the egg through, that's your job — the best part of the dish is doing it yourself.

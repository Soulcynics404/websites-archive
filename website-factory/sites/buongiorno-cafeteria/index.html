What we do

Three things, done properly.

No gimmicks, no eleven-page menu. Espresso pulled on a hand-lever machine, pastries baked in-house every morning and sold until they're gone, and seasonal brunch plates made to order all day.

  • Cornetti & bakes made in-house daily

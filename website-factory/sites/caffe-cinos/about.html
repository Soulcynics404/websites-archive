Our story

A family counter in Downham

Caffe Cinos is the little café on the corner of Old Bromley Road — run by our family, for yours, since the day we first switched the grinder on.

How it started

Built on regulars, not footfall

We opened Cinos because Bromley needed a café where the coffee is taken seriously but nothing else is. No laptops-only rules, no rushed turns — just a warm room, proper beans and people we know by name.

The family runs every shift. That means the person pulling your shot probably also did the baking at 5am, and will absolutely ask how your mum's doing.

What we stand on

01 · Real beans

Single-origin espresso rotated monthly from small roasters. If it's not good enough black, it doesn't make the menu.

02 · Fresh daily

Bakes come out before we open and when they're gone, they're gone. Bread from a Bromley bakery two streets away.

03 · Everyone's local

Dogs, buggies, running clubs and night-shift nurses finishing late. If you're kind, you belong here.

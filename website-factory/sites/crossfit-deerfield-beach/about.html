Our story

A BOX WITH A BACKBONE.

CrossFit Deerfield Beach started with a simple bet: coach people properly and the results — strength, confidence, community — follow on their own.

What we believe

EVERYONE LIFTS. THE BAR IS JUST DIFFERENT HEIGHTS.

The 65-year-old working on her first assisted squat and the Regionals athlete chasing muscle-ups belong in the same class here. Same workout, same hour, same coaching eyes on both.

We cap classes because attention is the product. We celebrate first pull-ups like competition PRs because to the person getting one, they are.

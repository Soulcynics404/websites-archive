Our story

Fifteen years on the same block.

Curry Heights opened its doors in 2009 with twelve tables, one tandoor and a family recipe book that crossed an ocean to get here.

The kitchen

Cooking is our first language.

The recipes are homestyle — the kind measured in fists and memory rather than grams. What you taste is generations of adjustment: a little more ginger here, a longer simmer there.

We kept the dining room warm and unpretentious because that's how we'd want to eat: good food, honest portions, no ceremony.

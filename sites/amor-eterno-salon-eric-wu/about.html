About Eric Wu

Trained editorial, grounded in real life

Eric spent his early career backstage at fashion weeks and on set — environments where a cut has to photograph from any angle and survive a twelve-hour day. He brought that discipline back to the salon floor.

Amor Eterno was born from a simple belief: great hair shouldn't feel rushed. One guest at a time, honest consultations, and cuts designed to grow out beautifully between visits.

  • Editorial & runway trained
  • Advanced color certification
  • Specialist in textured and fine hair alike
  • One guest per stylist, always

The hour, decoded

Every coach-led class runs the same proven structure.

  1. 00–10
    Brief + warm-up

    Whiteboard walkthrough, then dynamic prep for exactly what's coming.

  2. 10–25
    Strength / skill

    Barbell work or gymnastic skill with individual loading — technique before ego.

  3. 25–45
    The workout

    The conditioning piece, scaled to you. Coaches move the whole room through it together.

  4. 45–60
    Cool-down + wins

    Mobility, scores on the board, and someone's first pull-up celebrated properly.

How you can train here

Fundamentals

Three one-on-one sessions covering squat, press, deadlift, clean and the basic gymnastics vocabulary. Required for true beginners — worth it even if it isn't.

  • 3 × private sessions
  • Movement screen included
  • Rolled into first month

Group WODs

The heart of the gym. Coach-led hour, capped class size, whiteboard scores, scaled options printed right on the board. Six days a week.

  • Unlimited classes
  • Open gym access
  • Saturday team WODs

Strength Club

For members chasing bigger numbers: periodized barbell cycles, video review of your main lifts, and quarterly testing days that the whole gym shows up for.

  • Add-on to any membership
  • 2 coached platform sessions/wk
  • Meet-day experience

Nutrition Coaching

Habit-based guidance — not a meal-plan fax. Monthly check-ins, grocery walk-throughs and protein targets tuned to your training blocks.

  • Monthly 1:1 check-in
  • Body comp tracking
  • Built for real kitchens

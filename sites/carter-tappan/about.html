About Carter

A local first, an agent second.

I've spent my career in Palo Alto real estate — first learning the tracts from a bicycle, now representing buyers and sellers across the city as part of the Coldwell Banker Palo Alto office on El Camino.

My clients tend to be engineers, founders, professors and families who want data before adjectives. Every recommendation I make comes with the numbers behind it — days on market, price per square foot by tract, and what inspections actually found, not just what they cost.

How I work

  1. Advice before access.

    I'll tell you when NOT to buy. The lockbox will always be there next month.

  2. Data with every opinion.

    Comps, absorption rates and cost-to-curb models — in writing, before you decide.

  3. One client at a time on Sundays.

    Open homes are choreographed so your listing gets my full attention, not a divided one.

Neighborhoods I cover

From Professorville to Greenmeadow.

Old Palo Alto, Crescent Park, Professorville, Community Center, Midtown, Fairmeadow, Greenmeadow, Charleston Gardens, College Terrace — each behaves like its own micro-market. I track inventory week by week so you don't have to.

Thinking slightly farther afield? I also work Menlo Park, Atherton and east Mountain View.

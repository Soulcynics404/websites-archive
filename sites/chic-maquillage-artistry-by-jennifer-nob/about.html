About Jennifer

The artist behind the brushes.

Jennifer Noble has spent her career perfecting one skill: making people look like the most rested, radiant version of themselves on the most photographed day of their lives.

Calm hands, calm morning.

Brides describe the same thing after their wedding: the room felt peaceful. Jennifer times every appointment backward from your photographer's schedule, keeps the playlist mellow and the energy warm, and never leaves a face until it's flash-tested from three angles.

Her kit is built on skincare-grade primers and foundations chosen for Arizona's dry heat — makeup that survives a 12-hour day without turning cakey under the lights.

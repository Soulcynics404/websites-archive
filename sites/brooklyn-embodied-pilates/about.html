About

Built in a loft, taught like it matters

Brooklyn Embodied started with one reformer and a stubborn belief: Pilates isn't a trend class, it's a craft. Years later the loft has a full apparatus lineup and a roster of clients who move like they mean it.

Every instructor is comprehensively certified. No weekend certificates, no choreography roulette — just progressive, honest work.

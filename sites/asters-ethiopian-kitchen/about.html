From Addis to Austin

A kitchen that never rushed

Aster's began the way most good things do — family, recipes, and stubbornness. The wats here take hours because that's how long onions take to become sweet. The injera ferments for days because teff demands it.

For years we've been feeding downtown Austin: students discovering shiro, Ethiopian families who say it tastes like home, and regulars who plan their week around the lunch buffet.

  • Teff imported for authentic injera
  • Berbere blended in-house
  • Large vegan & gluten-free selection
  • Family-run, hospitality-first
Rated 4.5 stars by more than 400 diners — but the review we chase is “tastes like home.”
— Thank you, Austin

About us

Built for the neighborhood.

Center Street Cafe exists for the people who live and work downtown — a reliable table, a fast kitchen and coffee worth crossing the street for.

Treat everyone like a regular.

That's the whole idea. Learn the orders, keep the coffee fresh, get breakfast out fast on weekdays and slow down enough to talk on weekends.

We keep the menu short so the kitchen can move, the prices fair so you can come daily, and the room warm so you'll want to. The promenade gave us our name — the least we can do is give downtown Anaheim its living room back.

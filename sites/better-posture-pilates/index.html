The method

Why posture changes everything

When your spine stacks, everything downstream works easier — breathing, shoulders, hips, even your mood. Every session starts where you are.

01

Assess

Your first hour maps how you actually stand, sit and walk — not how the textbook says you should.

02

Align

Tailored apparatus work retrains the deep postural muscles that hold you up all day.

03

Maintain

You leave each session with a two-minute daily sequence so progress sticks between visits.

One-on-one attention

No crowded classes. Ever.

Sessions are capped at two people. Your instructor watches every rep, adjusts every spring, and keeps the work honest — that's how posture actually changes.

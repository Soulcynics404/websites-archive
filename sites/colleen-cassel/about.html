About me

The broker I needed when I bought my first place.

Buying in this city was confusing until someone sat me down and explained the whole board-package process on one legal pad. That afternoon is why I became an agent.

Today I run Cassel Real Estate as a boutique practice: fewer clients, deeper work. I've represented first-time buyers, growing families, downsizers and investors across Downtown Manhattan, the West Side and into Brooklyn Heights and LIC — and every one of them got my direct number, not a front desk.

Curated search

Board & closing support

Off the clock

A Long Islander in love with the city.

Long Beach summers, Manhattan weekdays. You'll find me walking the reservoir early, arguing about the best slice west of Sixth, and volunteering with housing nonprofits that help New Yorkers stay housed.

I believe homeownership should be an open door, not a gated mystery — which is why every client gets my plain-English buyer and seller guides before we ever meet a listing agent.

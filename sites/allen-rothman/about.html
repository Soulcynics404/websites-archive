About Allen

Two decades of New York. One specialty: homes that matter.

Allen Rothman is a licensed real estate sales agent with Compass in New York City. As part of the Horowitz Rothman Team — which he runs with his wife, Nina Horowitz — he specializes in the sale of estate properties: family homes, co-ops and condominiums whose sales carry legacies as well as price tags.

Based on the Upper West Side (10023), Allen works with executors, trustees, downsizers, and families across Manhattan who need a sale handled with patience, privacy, and precision — from pre-listing preparation through board packages and closing.

The Horowitz Rothman Team

Allen and Nina Horowitz lead a husband-and-wife team within Compass NYC. Nina's deep roots in New York residential sales complement Allen's methodical, client-first approach — together covering pricing strategy, staging, discreet marketing, and negotiation for every listing they take.

How an estate sale works with us

  1. Listen first.We start with the family's goals, timeline, and constraints — not the listing price.
  2. Prepare quietly.Clean-outs, staging, and small repairs coordinated end-to-end, with trusted vendors.
  3. Market discreetly.Public or quiet marketing — your choice. Many of our sales never appear on portals.
  4. Negotiate & close.Board packages, co-op/condo process, and estate attorneys managed to the finish line.

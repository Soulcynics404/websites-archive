About Coffee in the Wood

A neighbourhood café with proper beans and a soft spot for its regulars.

Our story

Born on the High Street, raised by regulars

Coffee in the Wood started with a simple frustration: Colliers Wood had plenty of places to grab coffee, but nowhere that treated it as craft without taking itself too seriously. So we opened our doors at 99–101 High Street with a secondhand roaster's worth of ambition and a lot of walnut panelling.

The name is a promise — this is coffee that belongs to the Wood. We hire locally, we know the school-run mums by order, we keep a jar of dog biscuits behind the counter, and we sponsor the football team nobody else would.

Six hundred and thirty Google reviews later (4.7★, thank you), the mission hasn't changed: make the best cup in SW19, then make people feel at home drinking it.

The craft

Small batches, dialled daily

Our espresso blend lands weekly from a small London roastery; single-origin guests rotate every Monday. Every morning before open, we dial in the grinder against a fresh shot and taste it black — if it's not right, we don't open until it is.

Milk comes from British family dairies, textured to silk for flat whites and cappuccinos. Oat, soya, almond and coconut are always free-of-charge swaps, because great coffee shouldn't be exclusive.

What we stand for

Three things we'll never compromise

☕

Coffee first, always

Speciality-grade beans only, roasted in small batches, dialed in every single morning. If a shot tastes off, it goes down the sink.

🤝

Neighbours, not customers

We remember your name, your usual, and how you take your eggs. Regulars built this place — we're just staff.

🥐

Fresh or nothing

Pastries arrive each morning before we unlock the door. Yesterday's stock never makes it to today's counter.

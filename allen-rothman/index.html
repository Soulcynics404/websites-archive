Estate properties are different: family homes, co-ops and condominiums whose sales carry legacies as well as price tags. Allen works with executors, trustees, downsizers and families who need a sale handled with patience, privacy and precision.

Based in the 10023 zip code, he runs the Horowitz Rothman Team at Compass with his wife, Nina Horowitz — her deep roots in New York residential sales paired with his methodical, client-first process.

How an estate sale unfolds

Selling a loved one's home is a project with feelings attached. The sequence below is the same whether the apartment is a $900K studio or an $8M Park Slope brownstone.

Start a conversation

  1. 01The family conversation. Heirs, executors and advisors aligned on goals, timeline and what stays private.
  2. 02Prepare the home. Clean-out crews, estate-sale professionals, painting and staging from trusted vendors.
  3. 03Pricing with proof. Comparable co-op and condo sales, board requirements and honest market timing.
  4. 04Closing the chapter. Offers, board packages, estate counsel coordination and the closing table.

Allen Rothman is a licensed real estate sales agent with Compass in New York City. As part of the Horowitz Rothman Team, which he runs with his wife Nina Horowitz, he specializes in the sale of estate properties: family homes, co-ops and condominiums.

Based on the Upper West Side (10023), Allen works with executors, trustees, downsizers and families across Manhattan who need a sale handled with patience, privacy and precision — from pre-listing preparation through board packages and closing.
